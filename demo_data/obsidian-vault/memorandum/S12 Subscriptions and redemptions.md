These terms are hypothetical fund conditions. A “business day” is a day when banks in London and Luxembourg are open and the markets needed to calculate NAV are operating normally. All times are Luxembourg local time.

| Item | Simulated operational term |
| --- | --- |
| Class I subscriptions | Every business Wednesday; if a holiday, the following business day |
| Receipt deadline | 12:00 noon on the business day preceding the subscription date |
| Availability of funds | Cleared funds and complete AML documentation required by the deadline; otherwise the order is deferred |
| Subscription price | NAV on the subscription date, determined after the receipt deadline |
| Redemptions | Last business day of each month |
| Redemption notice | 10 business days before the redemption date, by 12:00 noon |
| Redemption settlement | Normally within five business days of the redemption date |
| Lock up | No contractual initial lock-up period |
| Fund gate | Maximum 10% of outstanding shares redeemed on each monthly dealing date |
| Excess requests | Executed pro rata; balances carried to the next month without priority |
| Redemptions in kind | Excluded from the scenario |
| Share transfers | Subject to eligibility and AML checks on the new holder |

## Suspensions and investor protection

The board may suspend NAV calculation and dealing if a material part of the portfolio cannot be valued, relevant markets close, a serious cyber incident occurs or payments become unavailable. The manager informs investors within one business day of the decision, explains the reason and updates the communication at least weekly. No maximum duration is predetermined.

The gate does not guarantee liquidity by a particular date: repeated large requests may defer a redemption for several months. No arrangements give individual investors preferential liquidity. The scenario assumes that no suspension or gate has been applied between inception and 30 September 2026.

## Anti dilution adjustment

When the net flow on a dealing date exceeds 3% of NAV, the administrator may apply a price adjustment of up to 0.50%, based on expected costs and approved by the Valuation Committee. The benefit remains in the fund. It is neither revenue for the manager nor an ordinary entry or exit fee. The adjustment applied and its rationale are recorded.

Orders received after the deadline are carried to the first eligible date. The manager cannot unilaterally waive the deadline in favour of an investor. Conversions between classes are not offered in the scenario.
