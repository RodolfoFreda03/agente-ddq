## Essential definitions

NAV: assets less liabilities of a fund or share class. Net equity exposure: longs minus shorts, including equity futures. Gross equity exposure: the sum of the absolute values of both components. High water mark: historical net level that must be exceeded for a new performance fee to accrue. Gate: limit on shares redeemable on a dealing date. RTO: target recovery time. RPO: maximum data-loss window envisaged by the plan.
