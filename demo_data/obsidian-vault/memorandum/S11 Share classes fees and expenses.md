The following terms are entirely fictional. The classes share the same portfolio but have different costs and NAVs. Minimum amounts are not legal eligibility criteria. Class F is reserved for investors who entered during the initial period and has been closed to new subscriptions since 1 January 2025, including additions by existing investors.

| Term | Class I EUR | Class F EUR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Net assets at 30 September 2026 | EUR 288 million | EUR 72 million |
| Share of fund NAV | 80% | 20% |
| Minimum initial investment | EUR 1 million | EUR 5 million at launch |
| Minimum additional investment | EUR 100,000 | Not permitted since closure |
| Annual management fee | 0.85% | 0.60% |
| Performance fee | 15% of eligible excess profit | 15% of eligible excess profit |
| Simple annual hurdle | 4% | 4% |
| High water mark | Permanent for each subscription series | Permanent for each subscription series |
| Entry or exit fees | None | None |
| Income treatment | Accumulation | Accumulation |

## Fee calculation

The management fee accrues daily on class NAV before the accrual, using an actual/365 convention, and is paid monthly. Estimated ordinary operating expenses are 0.20% of NAV annually for both classes. The simulated contractual cap is 0.25%: the manager bears any excess ordinary expenses. Research and distribution are paid for by the manager.

The performance fee accrues daily and crystallises on 31 December and on redemption of an individual series. The threshold is the higher of the historical high water mark and beginning-of-year NAV increased by a simple 4% hurdle, prorated for time. The 15% fee applies only to the portion of NAV, after other costs and before the performance fee, that exceeds this threshold. An unmet hurdle is not carried forward; the high water mark is not reset.

Subscriptions are accounted for in separate series to avoid transferring fees between investors who entered at different times. Following crystallisation, the high water mark becomes the higher of its previous level and the series’ net NAV. Within the scenario, the administrator’s full methodology governs rounding and intraday allocations; its manual is not attached.

## Numerical example without cash flows

For a series with opening capital of EUR 1,000,000, a high water mark of EUR 1,080,000 and a year-end value after other costs of EUR 1,120,000, the hurdle is EUR 1,040,000. The higher threshold is EUR 1,080,000, making the performance fee base EUR 40,000. The fee is EUR 6,000 and the final net value is EUR 1,114,000. The new high water mark is EUR 1,114,000.

Trading, stock borrowing, financing and extraordinary expenses remain outside the operating expense cap. The totals of 1.05% for I and 0.80% for F represent only management fees plus estimated ordinary expenses: they are not total cost measures. Redemptions may involve an anti-dilution adjustment, described in S12.
