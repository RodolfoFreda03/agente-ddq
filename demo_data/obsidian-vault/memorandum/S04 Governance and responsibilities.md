The model separates investment decisions, risk control, valuation and payment authorisation. The portfolio manager may reduce risk immediately but cannot independently change limits, NAV or investor terms.

| Body | Scenario composition | Mandate and frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fund Board | 3 members, including 2 independent of the investment manager | Quarterly meetings; approves providers, terms, material conflicts and suspensions. |
| Investment Committee | Lead manager, co manager and Head of Research | Weekly meetings; discusses investment theses, position sizing and exits. The lead makes decisions within limits. |
| Risk Committee | CRO, COO, CCO and co manager | Monthly and ad hoc meetings for breaches; the CRO can block additional risk taking. |
| Valuation Committee | COO, administrator and independent director | Monthly meetings; validates pricing exceptions. The portfolio manager provides evidence without a final vote. |
| Conflicts Committee | CCO and two independent directors | Quarterly meetings; may exclude conflicted individuals and require corrections or disclosure. |

## Breach management

A breach caused by a new order is blocked before execution. A passive breach caused by market prices is reported to the CRO on the same day: the position cannot be increased and the team submits a plan within one business day. Compliance with the limit is normally restored within five business days, unless the Risk Committee makes a documented decision to avoid damaging forced sales. Every exception is recorded and reported to the board.

## Conflicts and order allocation

Orders shared across strategies are allocated before execution using predefined, proportional quantities. Average execution prices apply to equivalent aggregated orders; subsequent changes require justification and compliance approval. Transactions between funds managed by the firm are prohibited. Research is paid for by the investment manager rather than through soft commission arrangements charged to the fund.

Employees’ personal transactions require prior approval, a restricted list check and a minimum 30-day holding period, except for documented exceptions. Gifts exceeding EUR 150 require preapproval; those above EUR 500 are prohibited by the simulated policy. CRO and compliance remuneration does not depend on Aurora’s performance.

## Corporate oversight

The board annually assesses service provider independence, resource adequacy and team continuity. The scenario contains no signed minutes or actual attestations: the frequencies and powers described constitute the fictional governance model for this case.
