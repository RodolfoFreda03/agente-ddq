Aurora integrates environmental, social and governance factors into its assessment of economic risks and business quality. It has no sustainable investment objective, is not presented as an impact fund and assumes no SFDR classification, label or taxonomy alignment. The absence of those classifications in this case implies no judgement about any manager’s actual product.

## Strategy exclusions

The simulated policy excludes from long investments issuers directly involved in producing controversial weapons and those deriving more than 10% of revenue from thermal coal extraction. Thresholds apply to the latest available consolidated revenues and are reviewed quarterly. Excluded issuers cannot be held as direct shorts either, to avoid inconsistent treatment. Index hedges may create indirect exposures that are not screened.

## Analysis and controversies

Each investment note assesses governance, capital allocation, reporting quality, labour relations and environmental risks material to the sector. Internal ratings range from 1, low risk, to 5, high risk. A rating of 5 prevents new long positions pending a committee decision; existing risks require a documented plan and discussion with compliance.

The carbon metric does not offset long and short positions and excludes derivatives and Scope 3. The 90% coverage does not justify automatically estimating the remaining 10%. Figures are synthetic and do not come from an ESG provider. The scope definition must accompany any reuse of the metric.

## Voting and company engagement

The team favours informed voting, dialogue with management and verifiable objectives. An engagement with no progress after twelve months is reviewed to consider stronger engagement, voting against management or reducing the holding. These decisions do not guarantee environmental or social outcomes. Potential voting conflicts are assessed by compliance and, in material cases, by the board.

The fund’s proportion of sustainable investments, ESG certifications, alignment with climate scenarios and net zero commitments are not available. These must not be inferred from the processes described.
