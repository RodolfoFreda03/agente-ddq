## Capacity and asset growth

Indicative strategy capacity is EUR 750 million, assessed against current liquidity conditions and the opportunity set. At EUR 600 million, a formal review is triggered and may lead to subscriptions being closed. EUR 750 million is neither a fundraising promise nor a permanent ceiling: any increase requires risk analysis and board approval.

Simulated initial assets were EUR 80 million. The fund reaches EUR 360 million through performance and external flows; the memorandum does not contain a full historical subscription and redemption series. Asset growth therefore cannot be attributed solely to investment management. Side letters may address reporting and administrative requirements but do not provide preferential liquidity or fees different from the stated share class terms.
