Each investment is documented in a research note covering the thesis, valuation, catalysts, risks, proposed position and exit criteria. The archive retains versions, author and latest review date. The manager uses public sources, financial statements, company disclosures and authorised meetings; potentially inside information triggers a compliance check before trading.

| Stage | Activity | Documented output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1  Screening | Assessment of cash flow, returns on capital, indebtedness and stock liquidity | Watchlist of approximately 120 companies |
| 2  Analysis | Three-year financial model with base, upside and downside scenarios | Valuation estimate and sensitivity to key drivers |
| 3  Challenge | A second analyst checks assumptions, accounting and thesis risk | Independent review and recorded objections |
| 4  Control | ESG, concentration, liquidity, correlations and stock borrow checks | Risk opinion and pre trade checks |
| 5  Decision | Weekly discussion and position sizing by the portfolio manager | Authorisation and indicative maximum or minimum price |
| 6  Monitoring | Daily prices and news; monthly thesis review | Documented confirmation, reduction, exit or increase |

## Position sizing and risk management

A new long position normally starts at 1.5–3% of NAV; a direct short starts at 0.5–1.5%. Size depends on downside potential, liquidity and marginal risk contribution as well as conviction. S08 limits take precedence over the manager’s thesis. Index hedges allow rapid changes in risk without indiscriminately selling individual holdings.

The operating target for net exposure is 40–60%, within the wider 20–70% limit. The team reviews sector correlations and style factors weekly. Strong correlation between positions in different sectors may prompt a combined reduction; sector labels alone are not considered sufficient to measure diversification.

## Execution

Two traders, separate from the analysts, handle orders and document their choice of intermediary. Execution algorithms may participate in up to 15% of a stock’s expected daily trading volume, with lower limits in less liquid markets. Transaction cost analysis is monthly; the Execution Committee reviews prices, slippage, reliability and broker conflicts each quarter.

Annual equity turnover, calculated as the lower of purchases and sales divided by average NAV, is 68% for the twelve months ended September 2026. This metric excludes currency forward renewals and futures rolls. The research, screening and authorisations described are simulated; actual investment research notes are not available.
