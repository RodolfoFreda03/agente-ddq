## Valuation policy

NAV is calculated in euros on each business day using primary market closing prices. Listed equities use the official closing price and futures use the exchange settlement price. Currency forwards are valued by an independent source using current curves and exchange rates. Currency translation uses an independent 16:00 London fixing, with documented exceptions where necessary.

A price unchanged for two sessions, a daily movement exceeding 15% or a difference of more than 2% between sources triggers investigation. If no reliable price is available, the Valuation Committee approves a documented fair value. The manager may provide analysis but cannot unilaterally replace a price. All securities in the reference portfolio have quoted prices; no manual fair values are assumed.

## Errors and financial statements

Every NAV error is recorded, assessed and corrected. A difference equal to or greater than 0.25% of class NAV is an internal threshold for board escalation; it is not a regulatory threshold and does not rule out remedies below that level. The committee assesses the impact on all investors and any compensation required.

The scenario provides for audited annual financial statements within four months of year end and a half-year report within two months. The case assumes the 2025 process was completed on 24 April 2026, but includes neither financial statements nor an audit opinion: an “unqualified” opinion cannot be attributed to the fictional auditor.
