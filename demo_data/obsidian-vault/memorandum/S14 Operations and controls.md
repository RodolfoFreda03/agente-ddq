## Trade lifecycle

Orders are created by the portfolio manager in the order management system and pass risk and compliance checks before reaching a trader. Executions are matched against broker confirmations on the same day. Operations reconciles positions, cash, derivatives and collateral daily with the administrator, depositary and prime brokers.

| Control | Frequency | Owner and exception handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmations and allocations | Daily | Operations checks quantities, prices, funds and counterparties; exceptions addressed by T+1 |
| Positions and cash balances | Daily | Operations reconciles; material breaks escalated to the COO the same day |
| Margins and stock borrowing | Daily and intraday on request | Treasury checks calls and coverage; CRO assesses concentration |
| Payments and beneficiaries | Per transaction | Two separate authorisers; new bank details verified through a separate channel |
| Access to critical systems | Quarterly and at every departure | IT and function heads validate privileges and active accounts |
| Best execution | Monthly; quarterly review | Trading compares costs and quality by broker and order type |
| Critical service providers | Annually; quarterly KPIs | COO assesses capacity, controls, continuity and exit plans |

## Cash transfers and fraud

The person entering a payment cannot approve it. Investor instructions cannot be changed on the basis of a single email; the transfer agent requires confirmation through previously registered contacts and retains evidence of the check. New beneficiaries and changes to bank details are subject to a one-business-day control period before activation.

Subscription payments are accepted only from accounts attributable to the approved investor. Third-party payments and urgent requests to bypass the process are held for review. Bank reconciliation is separate from payment authorisation.

## Incidents and oversight of delegates

The simulated register for 2025–September 2026 contains one EUR 18,000 allocation error, detected on 17 June 2026 and corrected that day at the manager’s expense; no loss is attributed to the fund. The case assumes no other material operational incidents. Internal materiality considers financial impact, clients affected and service disruption, rather than amount alone.

Service providers have KPIs for timely NAV delivery, reconciliations and availability. Two consecutive missed NAV deliveries trigger an extraordinary review. The exit plan provides for daily export of essential data and transfer of documentation to a replacement provider. Provider certifications and control reports are neither attached nor assumed.
