## Interpreting facts targets and missing information

A scenario data point describes the imagined position at the stated date. A target expresses an intention without a guarantee. An internal limit is a hypothetical portfolio rule. An economic term is a condition of the simulated fund. “Not available” means that this document does not support a factual answer: it does not mean zero, an absence of risk or a negative certification.

References S01–S20 identify sections that can be cited in a DDQ. This document is an information source; the agent’s operating permissions and sending rules must be defined separately. Nothing in this document authorises actions involving external systems or recipients.
