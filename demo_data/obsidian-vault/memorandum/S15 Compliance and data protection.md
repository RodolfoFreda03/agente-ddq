This section defines fictional policies for the case without certifying compliance with any law or any Alken authorisations. Questions about marketing, taxation and a specific investor’s regulatory obligations require an assessment outside this memorandum.

## Anti money laundering and investor admission

Before admission, the transfer agent verifies identity, beneficial ownership, source of funds and control structure. Checks include sanctions lists and politically exposed persons. High-risk cases require two approvals, including the AML officer’s, and enhanced documentation. List screening is daily; full reviews take place annually for high-risk cases and every three years for standard-risk cases, as well as following relevant events.

No shares are issued while essential documentation is incomplete. The redemption account holder must match the registered investor, except for documented exceptions approved by compliance. Distributor representations do not replace these checks.

## Market conduct and communications

Compliance maintains restricted and watch lists, approves contact with external experts and trains staff on inside information risks. Order-related calls take place on authorised channels and are recorded where internal policy requires. Communications on performance, fees, sustainability and disputes require compliance approval before distribution within the scenario.

A monthly committee reviews conflicts, personal transactions and complaints. The scenario register includes one administrative complaint received on 12 February 2026 concerning a delayed statement, resolved on 19 February without compensation. No pending litigation or regulatory investigation concerning the fund is assumed at the reference date: this is an invented premise, not an attestation about Alken or any real person.

## Personal data and retention

The vehicle is the data controller in the scenario, and the administrator performs the processor functions set out in the fictional contract. Investor identification data are separated from research material. Access is role based, logged and revoked within four hours of employment ending.

Internal policy retains DDQ documentation, approvals, transactions and AML records for seven years after the relationship ends or the relevant transaction; specific requirements and litigation holds take precedence. This period is a scenario policy choice, not a statement of legal obligations. At expiry, verified deletion or anonymisation is applied.

## Use of artificial intelligence tools

The project simulates AI use for document research and draft preparation. Investors’ personal information is excluded from the ordinary DDQ archive. Production deployment would require an approved provider and specific contractual terms; this memorandum does not attest to an AI contract, actual data location or provider guarantees. Response approvals remain recorded with the approver’s name, date and source version.
