**Question:** What is the legal structure and domicile of the fund?

**Answer:** Alken Aurora European Equity Fund is an open-ended sub-fund of Aurora Strategies SICAV, domiciled in Luxembourg, with segregated assets. It is an alternative long/short equity fund intended for professional investors and is not presented as a UCITS. The vehicle manager is Meridian Fund Management S.A. and the delegated investment manager is Alken Asset Management (S02).
