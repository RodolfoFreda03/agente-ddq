**Question:** What is the investment universe of the fund?

**Answer:** The research universe comprises approximately 700 companies listed in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. The ordinary minimum market capitalisation is EUR 750 million, with at least EUR 5 million of average daily traded value over 60 sessions; up to 10% of NAV may be invested in smaller companies that meet liquidity requirements (S05).
