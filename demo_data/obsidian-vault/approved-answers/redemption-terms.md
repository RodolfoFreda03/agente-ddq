**Question:** What are the redemption terms of the fund?

**Answer:** Redemptions are monthly, on the last business day of each month, with 10 business days' notice by 12:00 noon. There is no lock-up. A fund-level gate limits redemptions to 10% of outstanding shares on each monthly dealing date; excess requests are executed pro rata and carried forward without priority (S12).
