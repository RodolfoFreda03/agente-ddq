**Question:** How often can investors subscribe to the fund?

**Answer:** Class I subscriptions are accepted every business Wednesday (or the following business day if it is a holiday). Orders must be received by 12:00 noon Luxembourg time on the business day preceding the subscription date, with cleared funds and complete AML documentation (S12).
