**Question:** On which days can investors subscribe to the fund?

**Answer:** Investors can subscribe to Class I shares every business Wednesday [S12]. If a subscription day falls on a holiday, the subscription occurs on the following business day [S12]. A "business day" is defined as a day when banks in London and Luxembourg are open and the markets needed to calculate NAV are operating normally [S12].
