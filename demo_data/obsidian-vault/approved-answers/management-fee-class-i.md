**Question:** What is the annual management fee for Class I?

**Answer:** The annual management fee for Class I EUR is 0.85%, accrued daily and paid monthly. A performance fee of 15% of eligible excess profit applies, subject to a 4% simple annual hurdle and a permanent high water mark per subscription series (S11).
