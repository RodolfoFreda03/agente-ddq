**Question:** How are payment instructions protected against fraud?

**Answer:** Payment instructions and cash transfers are protected by requiring that the person entering a payment cannot approve it, with transactions processed by two separate authorisers [S14]. New bank details and beneficiaries are verified through a separate channel and are subject to a one-business-day control period before activation [S14]. Additionally, investor instructions cannot be changed based on a single email, requiring confirmation through previously registered contacts [S14]. Furthermore, bank reconciliation is kept separate from payment authorisation, and third-party payments or urgent requests to bypass the process are held for review [S14].
