**Question:** Describe the trade lifecycle and reconciliation process.

**Answer:** Orders are created by the portfolio manager in the order management system and pass risk and compliance checks before reaching a trader. Executions are matched against broker confirmations on the same day. Operations reconciles positions, cash, derivatives and collateral daily with the administrator, depositary and prime brokers (S14).
