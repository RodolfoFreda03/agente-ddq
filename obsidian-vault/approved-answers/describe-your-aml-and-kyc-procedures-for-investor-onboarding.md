**Question:** Describe your AML and KYC procedures for investor onboarding.

**Answer:** Before investor admission, the transfer agent verifies the investor's identity, beneficial ownership, source of funds, and control structure, including checks against sanctions lists and politically exposed persons [S15]. High-risk cases require enhanced documentation and two approvals, including that of the AML officer [S15]. List screening is conducted daily, while full reviews occur annually for high-risk cases, every three years for standard-risk cases, and following relevant events [S15]. No shares are issued if essential documentation is incomplete, and distributor representations do not replace these checks [S15].
