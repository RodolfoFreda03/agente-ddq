**Question:** Describe the trade lifecycle and the reconciliation process.

**Answer:** Orders are created by the portfolio manager in the order management system, pass risk and compliance checks, and reach a trader [S14]. Executions are matched against broker confirmations on the same day, with operations checking quantities, prices, funds, and counterparties by T+1 [S14]. Operations also reconciles positions, cash, derivatives, and collateral daily with the administrator, depositary, and prime brokers [S14]. Material breaks in positions and cash balances are escalated to the COO the same day [S14].
