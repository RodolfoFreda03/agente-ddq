**Question:** What are your business continuity recovery objectives?

**Answer:** The recovery time objective for critical trading and reconciliation processes is eight hours and the recovery point objective for the document archive is four hours. These are plan objectives, not guarantees. The test on 15 May 2026 restored critical systems in six hours (S17).
