**Question:** Describe your research process from idea generation to investment decision.

**Answer:** The research process proceeds through six stages: screening companies into a watchlist based on financial metrics, conducting three-year financial modeling and analysis, undergoing an independent challenge by a second analyst, performing pre-trade risk and control checks, and reaching a final decision with position sizing during weekly discussions [S06]. Finally, the investment is continuously monitored through daily market tracking and monthly thesis reviews, with each investment fully documented in a research note [S06].
