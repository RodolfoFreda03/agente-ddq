**Question:** Describe the investment universe of the fund.

**Answer:** The research universe comprises approximately 700 companies listed in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland [S05]. The ordinary minimum market capitalization is EUR 750 million, with at least EUR 5 million of average daily traded value over 60 sessions [S05]. Additionally, up to 10% of NAV may be invested in smaller companies as long as they meet portfolio liquidity requirements [S05].
