The strategy involves potential loss of capital. Diversification, hedging and independent review do not eliminate risk; long and short positions may lose money simultaneously. Synthetic historical metrics do not demonstrate an ability to generate future returns.

| Material risk | Loss or disruption mechanism | Control and its limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selection and concentration | An incorrect thesis damages significant or correlated positions | Issuer limits and independent review; correlations may increase |
| Short selling | Price rallies, squeezes, higher borrowing costs or stock recalls | Limited position sizes and daily checks; short losses may exceed allocated capital |
| Market and factors | Index hedges do not replicate individual stock risks | Factor analysis and stress tests; basis risk remains |
| Liquidity and redemptions | Concentrated requests require sales in weak markets | Cash, notice and gates; redemptions may be deferred for a prolonged period |
| Counterparty and collateral | Insolvency or delays in recovering assets | Diversification and reconciliation; recovery is not guaranteed |
| People and processes | Lead manager departure, operational error or data loss | Succession, segregation and backups; residual risk remains |

## Illustrative stresses on the portfolio snapshot

| Hypothetical scenario | Simplified assumptions | Impact on NAV |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equity sell-off | Longs −15%; short equities −18%; short indices −20% | −5.85% / EUR −21.06 million |
| Losses on both sides | Longs −8%; short equities +20%; short indices +6% | −14.50% / EUR −52.20 million |
| Isolated currency shock | 10% loss on the entire 8% residual currency exposure | −0.80% / EUR −2.88 million |
| Collateral call | Additional margins of 5% of NAV, with no other flows | EUR 18 million cash requirement; not an automatic loss |

The first two impacts are calculated linearly on long weights of 95%, direct shorts of 30% and short futures of 15%. For the sell-off: 95% × (−15%) − 30% × (−18%) − 15% × (−20%) = −5.85%. Fees, slippage, currency movements and changes in exposure are excluded. No probabilities are assigned to the scenarios; they are neither forecasts nor maximum losses.

## Considerations before a hypothetical investment

The allocation thesis requires confidence in research quality, tolerance for double-digit losses and compatibility with monthly redemptions. An investor should be able to review constitutional documents, contracts, operational evidence and financial statements. Such evidence does not exist in the academic case: this memorandum supports a simulated initial DDQ review, not complete actual due diligence.
