## Investment liquidity

The liquidation estimate assumes sales or buybacks of up to 15% of average daily trading volumes over 60 sessions, open markets and no shock. Percentages show the cumulative proportion of the absolute notional of long equities and direct shorts, excluding cash and futures; they are not proportions of net assets immediately available for redemption.

| Liquidation period | Normal conditions | Trading volumes halved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Within one business day | 65% | 40% |
| Within three business days | 88% | 65% |
| Within five business days | 97% | 82% |
| Within ten business days | 100% | 97% |
| Within twenty business days | 100% | 100% |

The policy requires at least 90% of equity notional to be liquidatable within five days under normal conditions. The snapshot value of 97% meets this requirement. Concentrated redemption requests, suspended securities and stock borrow recalls may reduce effective liquidity. Unlisted positions and side pockets are prohibited.

## Financing and collateral

No structural bank borrowing is planned. A cash facility of up to 10% of NAV may be used for no more than ten business days to bridge settlement mismatches; utilisation at the reference date is zero. Short selling and derivatives nevertheless create economic leverage and margin obligations. Gross equity exposure is therefore not a comprehensive measure of financing risk.

Total cash and deposits are EUR 126 million: EUR 90 million restricted and EUR 36 million unrestricted. The liquidity plan includes a stress involving additional margin calls of 5% of NAV, or EUR 18 million. In that isolated scenario, opening unrestricted cash covers the requirement twice, before any redemptions or other simultaneous shocks.

| Aster Prime Securities | Prime brokerage and stock borrowing | 60% of short stock borrows; net credit exposure 4% of NAV |

The value of stock borrows is not the same as residual credit exposure after collateral. Legal recovery risk and collateral reuse risk remain. The scenario’s agreements prohibit reuse of fully paid long assets; margin treatment depends on the simulated contracts. Copies of those contracts are not available.
