## Calculation conventions and limitations

The annual return is the product of one plus each monthly return, minus one. The cumulative return uses all 45 months; the annualised return raises the cumulative growth factor to the power 12/45. Volatility is the sample standard deviation of monthly returns multiplied by the square root of 12. Drawdown compares each monthly NAV with the previous peak, including the initial NAV.

The 2026 data cover January–September only and are not annualised. Drawdown calculated monthly may understate intramonth losses. NAV per share does not determine fund assets without the number of shares and cash flows: asset growth is not the same as investment return. Gross returns, benchmark data, a Sharpe ratio and Class F performance are not available.
