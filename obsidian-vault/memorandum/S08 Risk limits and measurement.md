The CRO measures exposures, concentration, liquidity, currency risk and scenario losses daily. These limits are fictional internal rules for Aurora, not regulatory limits for a category of funds. The following metrics describe the position at 30 September 2026.

| Indicator | Limit or threshold | Simulated value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross equity exposure | Maximum 160% of NAV | 140% |
| Net equity exposure | Between 20% and 70% of NAV | 50% |
| Single long issuer | Maximum 7% of NAV | Highest observed 6% |
| Single direct short issuer | Maximum 3% of NAV | Highest observed 2.8% |
| Single long sector | Maximum 30% of NAV | Highest observed 24% |
| Five largest long positions | Maximum 30% of NAV | 26% |
| Residual currency exposure | Maximum 15% of NAV in aggregate absolute terms | 8% |
| 20-day historical VaR at 99% | Alert at 7%; not a maximum loss | 5.8% of NAV |
| 20-day expected shortfall at 99% | Alert at 10%; not a maximum loss | 8.2% of NAV |
| Drawdown from daily peak | Review at −6%; committee at −8% | −2.4% at the reference date |
| Unrestricted cash | Operating minimum 5% of NAV | 10% |
| Credit exposure per counterparty | Maximum 10% of NAV after eligible collateral | Highest observed 10% |

## Methods and frequencies

VaR and expected shortfall use a rolling three-year window of daily risk-factor movements applied to the current portfolio. The calculations include futures delta, currency risk and short positions. The underlying daily data are not included in the memorandum: VaR and expected shortfall are scenario values, not outputs that can be recalculated from the monthly series in S10.

The 8% residual currency exposure comprises GBP 4%, CHF 3% and Nordic currencies 1%. Other non-euro exposures are hedged with forwards. Total currency forward notional is 25% of NAV; it is excluded from the internal gross equity exposure metric of 140%. No regulatory measure of overall leverage is reported.

## Responses to events

Reaching the VaR threshold requires a same-day review, suspension of risk increases and an action plan from the portfolio manager. A drawdown of −8% triggers an extraordinary committee meeting within one business day to assess reductions in gross exposure and concentration. These thresholds do not establish a guaranteed loss level or prevent sudden market movements.

The CRO performs pre trade and post trade checks; the board receives a monthly report and a summary of exceptions. No active limit breach is assumed at 30 September 2026. The daily drawdown measure and the monthly measure in S10 use different frequencies and have different meanings.
