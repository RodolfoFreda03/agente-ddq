## Investment proposition overview

The fund seeks capital growth over a minimum five-year horizon by investing in undervalued European companies and taking selective short positions. Net exposure may range from 20% to 70% of net assets; the short book seeks to reduce market risk and profit from company-specific weaknesses. The strategy may lose capital even when hedges are in place.

The rationale is to combine fundamental stock selection, independent risk oversight and a portfolio largely capable of being liquidated within a few days. The principal trade-offs are reliance on the team, potential losses on both sides of the portfolio, short-selling risk and the ability to defer redemptions in exceptional circumstances.
