## Real biographical reference

Alken’s official website identifies Nicolas Walewski as Founder, Managing Director and Chief Investment Officer. Alken was established in 2005 as an independent boutique specialising in European equities. These are the only real biographical and corporate details used; no official assets under management, performance figures or organisational charts have been incorporated. ## Succession and alignment

The co portfolio manager may manage exposures within existing limits. An absence of the lead manager exceeding 20 business days triggers a board review and notification to investors within 5 business days of the decision. Team members collectively hold EUR 12 million in the fund; individual amounts are not specified. Under the simulated policy, 40% of the investment team’s variable bonus is deferred over three years.
