## Reconciliation to net assets

The simplified balance sheet comprises EUR 342 million of long equities and EUR 126 million of cash and deposits, less EUR 108 million of short equity liabilities: NAV EUR 360 million. Net derivative values and accruals are assumed to be zero in this snapshot. Cash is 35% of NAV, but EUR 90 million is restricted as collateral: unrestricted cash is EUR 36 million, or 10%. Futures notional exposure is not added to accounting assets.

Adding long and short exposures gives gross exposure of 140%; subtracting them gives net exposure of 50%. A position’s weight is not the same as its contribution to risk. The full list of 46 issuers is not included and must not be reconstructed from this snapshot.
